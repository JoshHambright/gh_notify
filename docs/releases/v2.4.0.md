# gh_notify v2.4.0

New-item toasts no longer pop up in front of an audience: they are held while you present, and you can pause them from the tray.

## Highlights

- **Hold toasts while presenting:** on Windows, toasts and sounds are held while a full-screen app, a game or presentation mode has the screen. On by default; turn it off in Settings › Notifications. [Learn more](../notifications.md#presenting-and-pausing-toasts)
- **Pause toasts for 1 hour:** a tray menu item that works for any screen share, including a Teams window share that Windows doesn't report. It survives a restart and ends on its own.

## New

- `notifications.holdWhilePresenting` in `config.json` ([Configuration](../configuration.md#notificationsholdwhilepresenting)).

## Changed

- Held toasts are dropped, not replayed afterwards. The items still appear in the popup and the badge.

## Fixed

- Nothing in this release.

## Upgrade notes

Nothing to do. If you share a window in Teams and still see a toast, use **Pause toasts for 1 hour** from the tray before you start.
